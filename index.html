Origin Story

From Deep
Below

Deep beneath the surface — past the roots, past the rocks, past the places light forgot — there exists a civilization that has never seen the sun.

The Diglett Mugs are the faces of that world. 5,000 souls who tunnelled up one strange night, poked their heads out of the dirt, looked around, and decided to stay visible.

Each one carries the marks of their underground life: hardened skin, wild hair, headgear forged in the deep mines, eyes that glow in shades the surface world has never named.

"We dug for years looking for something precious.
Turns out it was just us."

Some wear crowns salvaged from forgotten kingdoms. Some wear the helmets of deep-earth workers. Some just woke up with good hair. All of them are rare. None of them are going back underground.

Questions From The Surface

FAQ

What blockchain are Diglett Mugs on?
Diglett Mugs are minted on Ethereum. All metadata and images are stored permanently on IPFS — decentralized and not dependent on any server we run.
How many can I mint per transaction?
Up to 10 Diglett Mugs per transaction. There is no per-wallet cap — you can mint multiple transactions. This keeps individual mints fair while letting committed collectors grab more.
What size are the images?
Each Diglett Mug is a 512×512px pixel art PNG — OpenSea's recommended resolution. Sharp, crisp, and perfect for display. Every pixel is intentional.
Are the traits revealed at mint?
Yes. Diglett Mugs are fully revealed at the moment of mint — no blind boxes, no waiting. You see exactly what you're getting before you confirm the transaction.
Do I own the commercial rights?
Holders receive full commercial rights to their individual Diglett Mug. Use it as a PFP, put it on a t-shirt, build a brand around it — your Mug, your rules.
What makes a Mug rare?
Rarity is determined by trait combination. Crown headwear, red eyes, mic accessory, and the beard+moustache combo are among the rarest. Full rarity rankings published on Rarity.tools at launch.
